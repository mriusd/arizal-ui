Introduction

Arizal Exchange provides crypto prediction markets — timed Yes/No markets on crypto prices, settled in USDC on a single immutable exchange contract.

Market kinds

Every market is one of three kinds:

  • Hit — Yes if spot touches the barrier before expiry (and on related expiry rules).
  • Above — Yes if spot is at or above the barrier at expiry.
  • Between — Yes if spot finishes inside a low–high barrier band at expiry.

Timeframes start at daily, then weekly, monthly, and quarterly. Arizal does not offer, and does not plan to offer, short-horizon up/down markets (for example 5m or 15m). The shortest market is daily.

Philosophy

  • One immutable contract. A single-file smart contract, not upgradeable. What you see on chain is what runs; a change means a new deploy at a new address.
  • Simple enough to audit. Very small surface area so anyone — and any AI — can read it. Source is open on Etherscan.
  • Minimal UI. Few libraries, most application code written in-house, no unnecessary layers or frameworks for their own sake.
  • Home of crypto prediction markets. The product aim is to be the durable place people come for crypto-priced Yes/No markets — not a casino of minute-scale flips.
  • Independent and experienced. No VC investment. Bootstrapped by the dev team, with more than eight years building decentralized applications and multiple shipped decentralized projects.
  • AI for toil, humans for risk. AI helps with cumbersome work; core logic and contract security are written and audited by hand so exploits stay out of reach. Simplicity of the contract is a deliberate aid. That posture comes straight from EtherDelta — the original decentralized exchange — which remains secure to this day.
