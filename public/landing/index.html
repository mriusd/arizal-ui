Three kinds of markets

Daily is the shortest horizon. No 5m or 15m casino flips — by design.

  • Hit

    Yes if spot touches the barrier before expiry. The path matters.

  • Above

    Yes if spot finishes at or above the barrier at expiry.

  • Between

    Yes if spot finishes inside a low–high band at expiry.

Timeframes

Every market expires at an America/New_York midnight. Books run from the next daily close out to up to two years ahead — so you can trade near-term and longer-dated views on the same board.

  • Daily

    Next ET midnights. The shortest Arizal horizon.

  • Weekly

    Mondays 00:00 ET. Week-scale moves without minute-scale noise.

  • Monthly

    1st of the month 00:00 ET. Position for calendar months.

  • Quarterly

    Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct 1 ET. Longer tenors, up to ~2 years out.

How we build

  • One immutable contract. Single-file, not upgradeable. A change means a new deploy at a new address.
  • Simple enough to audit. Small surface area, open on Etherscan — readable by people and by AI.
  • Minimal UI. Few libraries, proprietary application code, no unnecessary layers.
  • Independent. No VC. Bootstrapped by a team with 8+ years shipping decentralized apps.
  • Security by hand. AI for toil; core logic audited by humans. The lineage is EtherDelta — still secure.
